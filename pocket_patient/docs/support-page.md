## PocketPatient — Support

PocketPatient is a clinical simulation app used exclusively by Rutgers
University for psychiatry coursework. Access requires a `@rutgers.edu` or
`@scarletmail.rutgers.edu` account.

**Need help signing in, reporting a bug, or have a question about how the
app works?**

Contact: tobiaat@rwjms.rutgers.edu

**Privacy questions or data requests:** see our
[Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) or contact the same address above.

**Common issues:**

- *Can't sign in* — confirm you're using your full `@rutgers.edu` or
  `@scarletmail.rutgers.edu` email. Other email domains are not
  supported.
- *Not seeing a case / course* — your professor controls when courses and
  cases are released to your account. Contact your course instructor if
  you believe you should have access.
- *Not receiving notifications* — check the app's Notification Settings
  screen, and confirm notifications are allowed for PocketPatient in your
  device's system settings.
